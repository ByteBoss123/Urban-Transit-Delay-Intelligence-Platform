Each trip is counted once per agency, route and service date in the trailing ten minutes. Its maximum reported stop delay determines whether it is delayed (≥180 seconds). A route is flagged at ≥35% delayed trips. This rule detects a concentration of delays; it does not establish causal propagation or predict future delays.
